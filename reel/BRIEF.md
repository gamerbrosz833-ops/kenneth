# Brief — Kenneth Rosales intro reel

- **Deliverable:** 12 s, 1920×1080 MP4 with sound, unnarrated. Embedded in the portfolio site and reusable as a LinkedIn featured video.
- **Category:** kinetic-type + abstract motion (asset-free).
- **Idea — "Signal over noise":** ~180 scattered dots (operational noise) organize into three lanes (a system), then compress into one champagne line (signal). Headline "Operations, made legible." then the name.
- **Style:** see `design.md`. Ink, ivory, one champagne accent. Instrument Serif + Inter + JetBrains Mono.
- **Sound:** synthesized in `tools/score.py` (no third-party samples): low pad, soft ticks as dots land, a hush on the snap to lanes, a bell on headline and name.
- **Facts used:** only from the CV (~200 cases/month, ~20 investigators, partners in India and Colombia).
